Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a painting project cost?

Every property is different, so we provide a free, detailed estimate after seeing the space (in person or via photos/video) — no surprise charges once work begins.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Burlington, NJ and serve homes and businesses within roughly a 50-mile radius, covering much of NJ and PA. See our Service Areas page for the full list.

How long does a typical job take?

A single room can often be completed in a day; full interior or exterior repaints typically take a few days depending on size and prep work needed. We'll give you a timeline with your estimate.

Do you help with color selection?

Yes — we're happy to offer color and finish recommendations as part of your consultation.

Not Sure Which Service You Need?

Tell us about your project and we'll recommend the right approach — free of charge.
