About MTO Painting

Our Story

Painting Done the Right Way

MTO Painting provides professional residential and commercial painting services throughout New Jersey and Pennsylvania. We specialize in interior and exterior painting, drywall repair, cabinet painting, trim painting, deck and fence staining, and power washing.

Based in Burlington, NJ, our skilled painters are dedicated to delivering quality workmanship, clean finishes, and reliable service — whether you need a single room painted or a complete property makeover. We show up on time, protect your space like it's our own, and don't consider a job finished until you're satisfied.
